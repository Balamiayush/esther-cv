I’m Rhyzza Mae Villasenor, a multidisciplinary designer exploring the intersection of culture, emotion, and aesthetics.

Born in the Philippines, raised in Italy, and now based in Germany, my work is shaped by contrast and connection. Moving between these cultures taught me to see design as a way to navigate identity, tell stories, and balance tradition with change.

I work across graphic design, branding, art direction, and UX/UI, creating visual identities that are both strategic and emotionally resonant. Influenced by my Filipino roots, the elegance of Italian design, and the clarity of German aesthetics, my approach is thoughtful, intentional, and human.
